About

Visionary
Architect

With over 18 years of experience, Ajay Singh Rathore creates architecture that resonates with context, culture, and future aspirations.

Based in Jaipur, India, his practice spans luxury residences, commercial complexes, cultural institutions, and sustainable urban developments. His work has been recognised internationally for its poetic minimalism and deep connection to the environment.
